Privacy Policy and Affiliate Disclosure

Updated November 10, 2026

Book Hotel And Flights (bookhotelandflights.com) is a planning guide that helps you choose a destination and a month before you search for a flight and hotel. It has no accounts or sign-ups. This page explains what is collected when you visit and how the site earns money.

How we earn money: Expedia affiliate links

The search boxes on this site and links marked "Expedia" are part of the Expedia Group affiliate program. When you search or click through, Expedia Group and its tracking partners may place or read cookies on your device so that a booking can be credited to us. If you book, we may earn a commission. You pay the same price as you would going to Expedia directly.

Your searches, bookings and payment are handled entirely by Expedia, under Expedia's privacy statement. We don't see your name, itinerary or card details. Commissions don't decide what we recommend; several of our pages explain when a package is the wrong choice.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which destinations and months people read about. It uses cookies to collect information such as pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location (city or region) and the site that sent you. We use it in aggregate and don't try to identify individuals. Our host, GitHub Pages, may also keep basic server logs for security.

Advertising

If we show Google ads, Google and other vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings and opt out of some vendors at aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. The guide still works without them; the Expedia search box may not. Questions: see the contact page.

Children and changes

This site isn't directed at children under 13. Changes to this policy update the date above.
