About Book Hotel And Flights

Updated November 10, 2026

Most flight-and-hotel sites start with a search box. This one starts with the question that matters more: where should you go, and in which month? Each destination guide covers the seasons, the areas to stay in, getting there and whether bundling the flight and hotel is likely to save money.

Who publishes it

Book Hotel And Flights is published by Eye To Ad Media, an independent web publisher in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We aren't a travel agency and don't sell or service bookings; searches and bookings go through Expedia.

How the guides are written

  • Season first. Hurricane seasons, dry and green seasons and holiday price spikes are the biggest factors in what a trip costs and feels like.
  • Honest about bundles. A package is often cheaper for a week at one resort and often not for a multi-stop trip. We say which.
  • Cautious on rules that change. Entry requirements and local fees change, so we point you to official sources rather than printing amounts that may be out of date.
  • No made-up prices or reviews. Live prices come from the search box, not from numbers we wrote months ago.

How it's funded

Through Expedia affiliate commissions when readers book, and possibly ads. Read the affiliate disclosure. Something wrong or out of date? Tell us.
